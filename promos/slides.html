Where the numbers come from

Counted three
different ways

Craft 5 keeps field values in three places. Count one and miss the others and the report is worse than useless.

  • Drafts and revisions excluded by default — fifty revisions per entry would call every abandoned field busy.
Most fields
elements_sites.content
Keyed by the field layout element’s UID. Read once per rebuild and tallied per field.
Relational fields
relations
Entries, Assets, Categories, Tags, Users. Both tables counted and the larger taken — which matters on a site upgraded from Craft 4 and never resaved.
Nested-element fields
entries.fieldId
Matrix, and CKEditor when it nests entries. These own entries of their own and store nothing in the content column at all.

Before you delete it

It reads your
codebase too

A field can be in no layout and hold no content and still be referenced — by a template that populates it on save, by a module, by a migration.

  • Every hit classified: entry.myField is worth more than the word in a comment.
  • File, line number and the source line, so “safe to delete” means something.
  • Every handle a field answers to — including the ones a field layout renamed.

What it won’t do

It installs
nothing. It
deletes nothing.

No tables, no writes, no migration to undo. Every screen is a question asked of what is already there.

  • Deleting a field destroys its content for every element, permanently — that decision belongs on Craft’s own settings screen.
  • With Joan’s cleanup list open next to it.
$ php craft joan/unused --fail-on=stranded
